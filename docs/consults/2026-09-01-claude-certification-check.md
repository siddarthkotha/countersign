## Key Findings

1. **Formal credentials exist but are restricted:** The four Claude Certifications (Associate, Developer, Architect Foundations/Professional) are official and proctored but limited to Claude Partner Network members. Public access details and pricing not disclosed on Pearson VUE's public site.

2. **Free learning available broadly:** Anthropic Academy offers 24 free courses covering technical topics (Claude Code, MCP, subagents, Agent SDK) but does not issue industry-recognized certifications—only completion certificates.

3. **DeepLearning.AI is the primary third-party partner:** Official Anthropic instructors teach courses on DeepLearning.AI platform (Elie Schoppik, Colt Steele). These are free, openly accessible, and explicitly partnered.

4. **Coursera course exists but partnership unclear:** A "Next-Generation AI Assistant: Claude" course exists on Coursera but the course page does not explicitly state Anthropic endorsement or partnership.

5. **No Udemy official endorsement:** Third-party Udemy courses exist but none are officially endorsed by Anthropic.
