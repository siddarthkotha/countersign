2 · The month from the brief's plan, with its pass/fail gates

Week 1 · Sep 1 to 7Kickoff Tuesday 10 AM. Build the skeleton end to end: a real call in, the checks, a verdict out, deployed at a web address.Gate G1 by Sep 7: a stranger can run the golden path unaided at the URL.
Week 2 · Sep 8 to 14The rule engine, the tools, the evidence record. Light week for you (NYC on the 10th); sessions continue.Gates G2 and G3 by Sep 14: the fake-CEO scenario runs twice in a row with no resets; verdicts reproducible; challenge questions sound natural.
Week 3 · Sep 15 to 21Polish the one path. The screen in the chosen look. The character. 50+ rehearsals with a live human attacker: interruptions, story shifts, pressure.Gate G5 by Sep 21: latency within target, or the stalling plan is redesigned.
Week 4 · Sep 22 to 28Video shoot, README, slides, cover, the recorded attack corpus. Submit Sep 27 or 28.Gate G6 by Sep 27: video has at least 2:20 of live agent interaction, no explainer longer than 15 s.
The founder's own law, already in the briefIf two gates slip, you make the downgrade call: ship as portfolio only, or do not ship. Winning is the credibility; a mediocre public entry is negative credibility. Your time: about two evenings a week plus the voice recording.
