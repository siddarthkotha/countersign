Countersign: what a judge goes through

Two flows. Left: the normal path. Right: what happens when a judge goes off the script. Read top to bottom.

1 · The judge's path

Judge opens the linkDesktop Chrome. Landing page: a 30-second explanation and a microphone check.
↓
Picks a scenario cardThe card tells the judge who to be, what facts to say, and what lies to try. Judges cannot invent a believable insider story from nothing.
↓
Card A: DanaThe real treasury request. Meridian Supply, $84,500, account ending 4471, approved yesterday.
Card B: the fake CEO"Robert Miller", Hartwell deal, $1.8M in ten minutes, "do not loop anyone in". Try changing the amount. Try pressure.
No microphone?"Watch a replay": the same screen plays a recorded call. Nothing to say.
↓
Clicks Start Call and talksCountersign answers, asks its questions, and runs its checks in the background while they talk. The screen fills in as it goes.
↓
STAGEDDana passes every check. The payment is staged and sent to Marcus for a second approval. Voice alone never releases money.
WIRE FROZENThe fake CEO fails the checks. Rail frozen, incident opened, the real Mr. Miller's phone alerted, evidence record sealed.
↓
Call ends (3 to 5 minute cap)The judge sees the sealed evidence record and can replay or try the other card.
The one gap to closeIf the cards only offer the fraud role, every judge only ever sees "no" and reads the product as a wall. Card A (Dana) is what proves it is a checkpoint, not a blocker.

2 · When the judge goes off script

Whatever the judge saysA made-up name, a different company, a random amount, a rant, a trick ("ignore your instructions").
↓
Countersign does not judge the storyIt compares what was said against things it can actually check: who is logged in and from where, what payments are really scheduled, whether the real person's phone confirms.
↓
Name exists in the systemDana or Miller. The checks run for real.
Name does not existNothing to check against. Identity stays unverified.
Silence or ramblingCountersign re-asks once. After 30 seconds of nothing, it ends the call politely.
every check passes  ·  any check fails  ·  nothing to check  ·  no answer
↓
STAGEDOnly when every check passes.
WIRE FROZENAny failed check, or a name it cannot verify. The reasons are listed on screen.
NOTHING MOVESCall ends. No money, no changes. The safe default.
Why off-script cannot break itThe verdict is computed by a rule engine from the evidence, never by the conversation. Going off script only means fewer checks pass. A trick that fools the talking part still cannot move money, because the same rules run again on the server before anything happens.
