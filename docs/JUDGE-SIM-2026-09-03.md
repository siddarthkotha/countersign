## Findings and what happened to each

1. CRITICAL. The legitimate scenario, the first and easiest one the judge is invited to try,
   can loop for two and a half minutes and time out with no verdict. Root-caused the same
   night to two separate bugs; both fixes dispatched. See docs/RED-TEAM-2026-09-03.md.
2. CRITICAL. Knowledge challenges are not scoped to the caller, so an honest caller can be
   asked a question about a deal she has nothing to do with and can never answer. Fix
   dispatched (FIX8).
3. IMPORTANT. The replay corpus cannot catch live-selection bugs by construction: it validates
   a recorded challenge choice as legal instead of re-deriving it from the live selector, so
   18 of 18 stays green while live selection is broken. PARKED: changing how the oracle works
   is a founder decision, not an autopilot one.
4. IMPORTANT. Tool calling, the voice API's most distinctive agent feature, is switched off in
   the shipped build with no explanation a judge can see. It was switched off for a good
   reason on a live call. Honest trade-off paragraph dispatched (D2).
5. IMPORTANT. The submission tells judges to click Replay first, and the screen hands them an
   unlabelled alphabetical dropdown of 18 raw filenames with the flagship recording 14th.
   Fix dispatched (W10).
6. IMPORTANT. The free plan sleeps and can take about a minute to wake, and neither entry
   point says anything while that happens, so a stranger cannot tell waking from broken.
   Fix dispatched (W10).
7. IMPORTANT. The prior-art citation and the "we claim the application, not the pattern"
   framing live only in the internal brief, never in the README or the submission draft.
   Fix dispatched (D2).
8. MINOR. The video hook says "last year" about a February 2024 event, in a September 2026
   submission. Fix dispatched (D2); the same line in the brief needs it too.
9. MINOR. No quantified business figure anywhere in the submission draft. Dispatched (D2)
   under the truth rule: a sourced number, a labelled estimate with its method, or an explicit
   unknown. Never an invented figure.
10. MINOR. The naming collision with an unrelated product on the plain address. Already CLOSED
